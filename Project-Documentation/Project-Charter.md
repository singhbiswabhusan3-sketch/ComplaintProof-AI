# ComplaintProof AI — Project Charter

## Project Overview
ComplaintProof AI is a business analysis portfolio project focused on investigating food delivery complaints and identifying operational gaps using data.

## Business Problem
Customers have reported delays in food delivery. The project investigates potential causes across restaurant preparation, delivery partner assignment, and delivery operations.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze food delivery complaint data.
- Identify operational bottlenecks.
- Validate data quality.
- Define measurable business metrics.
- Recommend data-driven improvements.
